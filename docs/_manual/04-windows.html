Title bar

The round colored buttons at the top left always show their symbol: × closes, − minimizes, the arrows maximize or restore. Double-click the title bar to maximize or restore.

Settings → Desktop & Dock → Windows changes, in both sessions and for new windows:

  • Buttons: on the left (close, minimize, maximize) or on the right as on Windows (minimize, maximize, close).
  • Button style: colored circles or monochrome icons.
  • Corner radius: from square (0) to very round (18 px); 14 px to begin with. It applies to the window an app draws itself, to the borders the compositor draws around apps that don't, and to the title bar.
  • Gaps around snapped windows: space left around windows snapped to a half, third or quarter (keyboard, dragging to an edge, Snap Layouts and Snap Assist), the same between windows as along the screen's edges. Maximized windows keep the whole screen.

Apps already open take the new buttons and corners when they are opened again.

Moving and resizing

  • Drag the title bar to move; drag an edge or corner to resize.
  • Hold Super and drag anywhere in a window with the left button to move it, or with the right button to resize it.
  • Drag a window to the left or right edge of the screen to snap it to that half, to the top to maximize it.

Switching

  • Alt+Tab (or Super+Tab) moves to the next window, Alt+Shift+Tab to the previous.
  • The window buttons in the top bar, the dock, and the overview (Super+W).

When the resolution changes

When a display's resolution or scale changes, floating windows keep their relative place and size on it instead of hanging off the screen; maximized, snapped and fullscreen windows follow by themselves. Desktop widgets keep their places too.

Animations

In the Aurora session, windows fade and zoom as they open, close, minimize and come back. Settings → Appearance → Window animations turns them off (together with Files' transitions); Animations turns off every interface animation. See Desktop sessions.

Snap Layouts

Press Super+Z. A panel at the top of the screen shows six layouts:

  • two halves;
  • two thirds and one third;
  • three thirds;
  • a half and two quarters;
  • four quarters;
  • a wide middle between two narrow columns.

Point at a zone in a layout: the screen shows where the window will go. Click: the window in front moves there. Esc or a click elsewhere closes the panel.

Snap Assist

Right after you place a window, every empty zone is outlined and the first one lists your other windows (most recently used first). Click one to put it in that zone; then the next zone asks, until the layout is full. Leave Empty skips a zone, Esc stops.

Snap Assist also appears when you snap a window to a half with Super+← or → (or by dragging it to the edge), offering the other half, unless a window is already snapped there.

Without a window in front, choosing a zone in Snap Layouts asks for a window for every zone, starting with the one you clicked.

Snap Layouts and Snap Assist need the Aurora (Wayfire) session. In Aurora Compatibility the keyboard snapping above still works.

Press Super+T to keep the window in front above every other window, even when you click elsewhere (a video, a calculator, notes during a call). An indicator confirms it. Press Super+T again in the same window to stop.

The Aurora Assistant always floats above other windows and on every workspace.
