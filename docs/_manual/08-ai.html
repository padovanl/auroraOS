Aurora AI is off until you turn it on in Settings → AI. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is sent anywhere before that.

1. Choose where answers come from

This computer (private)
Download a model; it runs locally, even offline, and nothing leaves the computer. The model loads when you ask and unloads after 10 idle minutes. Suggested models:
ModelFor
Qwen3 1.7BAny computer
Qwen3 4B, Gemma 3 4B8 GB of memory
Qwen3 8B16 GB of memory
It runs with llama.cpp's server, downloaded on demand and verified by checksum. A graphics card speeds it up when the driver supports Vulkan.
Your network
Ollama or LM Studio on this or another computer: choose OpenAI-compatible API and enter its address, for example http://192.168.1.20:11434/v1 for Ollama or http://192.168.1.20:1234/v1 for LM Studio (Ollama on this computer fills it in for the local one). Attachments are allowed with these, since nothing leaves your network.
A cloud provider
Anthropic Claude, or an OpenAI-compatible service (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, OpenRouter, Groq). Paste your API key; it is kept in your keyring.

API keys are billed per use by the provider, separately from a Claude Pro/Max or ChatGPT Plus subscription, which other apps can't use. To use a subscription, install Claude Code or Codex from Dev Hub → AI Assistants: they sign in with your plan.

2. Choose where it helps

Each place has its own switch in Settings → AI. See Where Aurora AI helps.

3. Languages

It answers in the language you write in and follows “reply in Italian” whatever the system language. You can also fix one language for answers and one for dictation (or let it detect), and download a reading voice for any of 19 languages.

Privacy

  • With a model on your computer, nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Files and photos you attach are only sent to a model on your computer or your network, never to a cloud provider.
  • A chat that used private attachments can't continue with a cloud provider: start a new chat.
  • Chats are saved on this computer.

Open the Assistant from the dock, the ✦ in the top bar, or with Super+Shift+Space.

Where it sits

The Assistant floats like a picture-in-picture video: a card in the bottom-right corner, above your other windows and on every workspace. It sits just above the dock when the dock is under it, and right at the bottom of the screen when the dock is elsewhere, hidden or ends to its left. It never takes the keyboard from the app you're using unless you click it or open it with the shortcut.

  • ↓ collapses it to a small bar, like a chat on a web page; a dot shows when a reply arrives.
  • ⤢ makes it bigger for long answers.
  • × closes it.

Chatting

  • Type and press Enter. Answers stream in; Stop ends one, and you can regenerate it.
  • Code blocks have a copy button; answers can be read aloud.
  • New replies scroll into view unless you're reading earlier messages: then a highlighted arrow takes you to the latest.
  • + starts a new chat, the clock icon opens earlier chats.
  • The model is chosen from the title (for example On this computer).

Attachments

The paperclip opens a file chooser (the Assistant collapses while it's open); you can also drag files into the chat. Text, code, documents (PDF, office files) and photos are supported; photos are read with offline text recognition. Up to four attachments per message. Attachments only go to a model on your computer or your network.

Quick actions

Summarize, Improve Writing, Translate and Explain work on, in this order:

  1. the text you typed or pasted in the Assistant's box;
  2. otherwise, the attached files;
  3. otherwise, the text you last copied (or selected) in any app.
